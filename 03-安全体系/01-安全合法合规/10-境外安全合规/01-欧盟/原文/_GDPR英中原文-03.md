### 第III章 数据主体权利 / CHAPTER III — Rights of the data subject {#gdpr-chapter-iii}

**第1节 透明度与行使方式 / Section 1 — Transparency and modalities**

<details id="gdpr-article-12">
<summary>第12条 数据主体行使权利时的透明信息、沟通与方式 / Article 12 — Transparent information, communication and modalities for the exercise of the rights of the data subject</summary>

**English**

1. The controller shall take appropriate measures to provide any information referred to in Articles 13 and 14 and any communication under Articles 15 to 22 and 34 relating to processing to the data subject in a concise, transparent, intelligible and easily accessible form, using clear and plain language, in particular for any information addressed specifically to a child. The information shall be provided in writing, or by other means, including, where appropriate, by electronic means. When requested by the data subject, the information may be provided orally, provided that the identity of the data subject is proven by other means.

2. The controller shall facilitate the exercise of data subject rights under Articles 15 to 22. In the cases referred to in Article 11(2), the controller shall not refuse to act on the request of the data subject for exercising his or her rights under Articles 15 to 22, unless the controller demonstrates that it is not in a position to identify the data subject.

3. The controller shall provide information on action taken on a request under Articles 15 to 22 to the data subject without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt of the request. That period may be extended by two further months where necessary, taking into account the complexity and number of the requests. The controller shall inform the data subject of any such extension within one month of receipt of the request, together with the reasons for the delay. Where the data subject makes the request by electronic form means, the information shall be provided by electronic means where possible, unless otherwise requested by the data subject.

4. If the controller does not take action on the request of the data subject, the controller shall inform the data subject without delay and at the latest within one month of receipt of the request of the reasons for not taking action and on the possibility of lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority and seeking a judicial remedy.

5. Information provided under Articles 13 and 14 and any communication and any actions taken under Articles 15 to 22 and 34 shall be provided free of charge. Where requests from a data subject are manifestly unfounded or excessive, in particular because of their repetitive character, the controller may either:

(a) charge a reasonable fee taking into account the administrative costs of providing the information or communication or taking the action requested; or

(b) refuse to act on the request.

The controller shall bear the burden of demonstrating the manifestly unfounded or excessive character of the request.

6. Without prejudice to Article 11, where the controller has reasonable doubts concerning the identity of the natural person making the request referred to in Articles 15 to 21, the controller may request the provision of additional information necessary to confirm the identity of the data subject.

7. The information to be provided to data subjects pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 may be provided in combination with standardised icons in order to give in an easily visible, intelligible and clearly legible manner a meaningful overview of the intended processing. Where the icons are presented electronically they shall be machine-readable.

8. The Commission shall be empowered to adopt delegated acts in accordance with Article 92 for the purpose of determining the information to be presented by the icons and the procedures for providing standardised icons.

**中文参考译文**

1. 控制者应采取适当措施，以简洁、透明、易懂且易于获取的形式，使用清楚、平实的语言，向数据主体提供第13、14条所述的任何信息，以及第15至22条和第34条规定的与处理有关的任何沟通内容，特别是专门面向儿童的信息。信息应以书面或其他方式提供，包括在适当情况下以电子方式提供。应数据主体请求，可以口头提供信息，但须通过其他方式证明数据主体的身份。

2. 控制者应便利数据主体行使第15至22条规定的权利。在第11条第2款所述情况下，控制者不得拒绝就数据主体行使第15至22条权利的请求采取行动，除非控制者能够证明其无法识别数据主体的身份。

3. 控制者应无不当迟延地向数据主体提供其就第15至22条请求采取行动的信息，并且无论如何应在收到请求后一个月内提供。考虑请求的复杂性和数量，必要时该期限可以再延长两个月。控制者应在收到请求后一个月内，将任何此类延期及迟延原因告知数据主体。数据主体以电子方式提出请求的，应尽可能以电子方式提供信息，但数据主体另有请求的除外。

4. 控制者不就数据主体的请求采取行动的，应及时且最迟在收到请求后一个月内，告知数据主体不采取行动的原因，以及向监管机构投诉和寻求司法救济的可能途径。

5. 依据第13、14条提供的信息，以及依据第15至22条和第34条进行的任何沟通和采取的任何行动，均应免费。数据主体的请求明显缺乏依据或过度，特别是具有重复性的，控制者可以：

(a) 考虑提供信息、进行沟通或采取所请求行动的行政成本，收取合理费用；或者

(b) 拒绝就请求采取行动。

控制者应承担证明请求明显缺乏依据或过度的责任。

6. 在不影响第11条的前提下，控制者对提出第15至21条所述请求的自然人身份存在合理怀疑的，可以要求提供确认数据主体身份所必需的额外信息。

7. 依据第13、14条向数据主体提供的信息，可以结合标准化图标呈现，以易见、易懂且清晰可读的方式，对拟进行的处理给出具有实际意义的概览。以电子形式呈现的图标，应可供机器读取。

8. 欧盟委员会有权依据第92条制定授权法案，以确定图标应呈现的信息及提供标准化图标的程序。

</details>

**第2节 信息和访问个人数据 / Section 2 — Information and access to personal data**

<details id="gdpr-article-13">
<summary>第13条 向数据主体收集个人数据时应提供的信息 / Article 13 — Information to be provided where personal data are collected from the data subject</summary>

**English**

1. Where personal data relating to a data subject are collected from the data subject, the controller shall, at the time when personal data are obtained, provide the data subject with all of the following information:

(a) the identity and the contact details of the controller and, where applicable, of the controller's representative;

(b) the contact details of the data protection officer, where applicable;

(c) the purposes of the processing for which the personal data are intended as well as the legal basis for the processing;

(d) where the processing is based on point (f) of Article 6(1), the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party;

(e) the recipients or categories of recipients of the personal data, if any;

(f) where applicable, the fact that the controller intends to transfer personal data to a third country or international organisation and the existence or absence of an adequacy decision by the Commission, or in the case of transfers referred to in Article 46 or 47, or the second subparagraph of Article 49(1), reference to the appropriate or suitable safeguards and the means by which to obtain a copy of them or where they have been made available.

2. In addition to the information referred to in paragraph 1, the controller shall, at the time when personal data are obtained, provide the data subject with the following further information necessary to ensure fair and transparent processing:

(a) the period for which the personal data will be stored, or if that is not possible, the criteria used to determine that period;

(b) the existence of the right to request from the controller access to and rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction of processing concerning the data subject or to object to processing as well as the right to data portability;

(c) where the processing is based on point (a) of Article 6(1) or point (a) of Article 9(2), the existence of the right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal;

(d) the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority;

(e) whether the provision of personal data is a statutory or contractual requirement, or a requirement necessary to enter into a contract, as well as whether the data subject is obliged to provide the personal data and of the possible consequences of failure to provide such data;

(f) the existence of automated decision-making, including profiling, referred to in Article 22(1) and (4) and, at least in those cases, meaningful information about the logic involved, as well as the significance and the envisaged consequences of such processing for the data subject.

3. Where the controller intends to further process the personal data for a purpose other than that for which the personal data were collected, the controller shall provide the data subject prior to that further processing with information on that other purpose and with any relevant further information as referred to in paragraph 2.

4. Paragraphs 1, 2 and 3 shall not apply where and insofar as the data subject already has the information.

**中文参考译文**

1. 直接向数据主体收集与其有关的个人数据时，控制者应在获取个人数据之时，向数据主体提供下列全部信息：

(a) 控制者的身份和联系方式，以及适用时控制者代表的身份和联系方式；

(b) 适用时，数据保护官的联系方式；

(c) 个人数据拟用于何种处理目的，以及处理的法律依据；

(d) 处理基于第6条第1款(f)项的，控制者或第三方所追求的正当利益；

(e) 如有，个人数据的接收方或接收方类别；

(f) 适用时，控制者拟将个人数据传输至第三国或国际组织的事实，以及欧盟委员会是否作出充分性决定；对于第46、47条或第49条第1款第二分段所述传输，应指明适当或适宜的保障措施，以及获得其副本的方式或公开获取位置。

2. 除第1款所述信息外，控制者还应在获取个人数据之时，向数据主体提供确保公平、透明处理所必需的下列补充信息：

(a) 个人数据将保存的期限；无法确定的，提供用于确定该期限的标准；

(b) 有权请求控制者提供对与数据主体有关的个人数据的访问、更正或删除、限制处理，或反对处理，以及享有数据可携带权；

(c) 处理基于第6条第1款(a)项或第9条第2款(a)项的，有权随时撤回同意，且不影响撤回前基于同意进行处理的合法性；

(d) 向监管机构投诉的权利；

(e) 提供个人数据是否属于法定或合同要求，或订立合同所必需的要求；数据主体是否有义务提供个人数据，以及未提供数据可能产生的后果；

(f) 是否存在第22条第1、4款所述的自动化决策，包括画像；并至少在该等情况下，提供关于所涉及逻辑的有意义信息，以及该处理对数据主体的意义和预期后果。

3. 控制者拟为不同于收集目的的其他目的进一步处理个人数据的，应在进一步处理之前，将该其他目的及第2款所述任何相关补充信息提供给数据主体。

4. 在数据主体已经掌握该等信息的情况下，并在其已掌握的范围内，第1、2、3款不适用。

</details>

<details id="gdpr-article-14">
<summary>第14条 未从数据主体取得个人数据时应提供的信息 / Article 14 — Information to be provided where personal data have not been obtained from the data subject</summary>

**English**

1. Where personal data have not been obtained from the data subject, the controller shall provide the data subject with the following information:

(a) the identity and the contact details of the controller and, where applicable, of the controller's representative;

(b) the contact details of the data protection officer, where applicable;

(c) the purposes of the processing for which the personal data are intended as well as the legal basis for the processing;

(d) the categories of personal data concerned;

(e) the recipients or categories of recipients of the personal data, if any;

(f) where applicable, that the controller intends to transfer personal data to a recipient in a third country or international organisation and the existence or absence of an adequacy decision by the Commission, or in the case of transfers referred to in Article 46 or 47, or the second subparagraph of Article 49(1), reference to the appropriate or suitable safeguards and the means to obtain a copy of them or where they have been made available.

2. In addition to the information referred to in paragraph 1, the controller shall provide the data subject with the following information necessary to ensure fair and transparent processing in respect of the data subject:

(a) the period for which the personal data will be stored, or if that is not possible, the criteria used to determine that period;

(b) where the processing is based on point (f) of Article 6(1), the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party;

(c) the existence of the right to request from the controller access to and rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction of processing concerning the data subject and to object to processing as well as the right to data portability;

(d) where processing is based on point (a) of Article 6(1) or point (a) of Article 9(2), the existence of the right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal;

(e) the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority;

(f) from which source the personal data originate, and if applicable, whether it came from publicly accessible sources;

(g) the existence of automated decision-making, including profiling, referred to in Article 22(1) and (4) and, at least in those cases, meaningful information about the logic involved, as well as the significance and the envisaged consequences of such processing for the data subject.

3. The controller shall provide the information referred to in paragraphs 1 and 2:

(a) within a reasonable period after obtaining the personal data, but at the latest within one month, having regard to the specific circumstances in which the personal data are processed;

(b) if the personal data are to be used for communication with the data subject, at the latest at the time of the first communication to that data subject; or

(c) if a disclosure to another recipient is envisaged, at the latest when the personal data are first disclosed.

4. Where the controller intends to further process the personal data for a purpose other than that for which the personal data were obtained, the controller shall provide the data subject prior to that further processing with information on that other purpose and with any relevant further information as referred to in paragraph 2.

5. Paragraphs 1 to 4 shall not apply where and insofar as:

(a) the data subject already has the information;

(b) the provision of such information proves impossible or would involve a disproportionate effort, in particular for processing for archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical research purposes or statistical purposes, subject to the conditions and safeguards referred to in Article 89(1) or in so far as the obligation referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article is likely to render impossible or seriously impair the achievement of the objectives of that processing. In such cases the controller shall take appropriate measures to protect the data subject's rights and freedoms and legitimate interests, including making the information publicly available;

(c) obtaining or disclosure is expressly laid down by Union or Member State law to which the controller is subject and which provides appropriate measures to protect the data subject's legitimate interests; or

(d) where the personal data must remain confidential subject to an obligation of professional secrecy regulated by Union or Member State law, including a statutory obligation of secrecy.

1. 个人数据并非从数据主体取得的，控制者应向数据主体提供下列信息：

(a) 控制者的身份和联系方式，以及适用时控制者代表的身份和联系方式；

(b) 适用时，数据保护官的联系方式；

(c) 个人数据拟用于何种处理目的，以及处理的法律依据；

(d) 所涉及的个人数据类别；

(e) 如有，个人数据的接收方或接收方类别；

(f) 适用时，控制者拟将个人数据传输至第三国或国际组织内接收方的事实，以及欧盟委员会是否作出充分性决定；对于第46、47条或第49条第1款第二分段所述传输，应指明适当或适宜的保障措施，以及获得其副本的方式或公开获取位置。

(a) 个人数据将保存的期限；无法确定的，提供用于确定该期限的标准；

(b) 处理基于第6条第1款(f)项的，控制者或第三方追求的正当利益；

(c) 有权请求控制者提供对与数据主体有关的个人数据的访问、更正或删除、限制处理，以及反对处理，并享有数据可携带权；

(d) 处理基于第6条第1款(a)项或第9条第2款(a)项的，有权随时撤回同意，且不影响撤回前基于同意进行处理的合法性；

(e) 向监管机构投诉的权利；

(f) 个人数据的来源，以及适用时是否来自公众可访问的来源；

(g) 是否存在第22条第1、4款所述自动化决策，包括画像；并至少在该等情况下，提供关于所涉及逻辑的有意义信息，以及该处理对数据主体的意义和预期后果。

3. 控制者应在下列期限提供第1、2款所述信息：

(a) 考虑个人数据处理的具体情况，在取得个人数据后的合理期间内，但最迟不超过一个月；

(b) 个人数据拟用于与数据主体沟通的，最迟在首次与该数据主体沟通之时；或者

(c) 拟向其他接收方披露个人数据的，最迟在首次披露个人数据之时。

4. 控制者拟为不同于取得目的的其他目的进一步处理个人数据的，应在进一步处理之前，将该其他目的及第2款所述任何相关补充信息提供给数据主体。

5. 在下列情况下，并限于该情况所及范围，第1至4款不适用：

(a) 数据主体已掌握该等信息；

(b) 提供该等信息经证明不可能，或需要付出不相称的努力，特别是在遵守第89条第1款所述条件和保障措施的前提下，为公共利益档案保存、科学或历史研究或统计目的进行处理；或者本条第1款所述义务可能导致处理目的无法实现或严重妨碍其实现。在该等情况下，控制者应采取适当措施保护数据主体的权利、自由和正当利益，包括将该等信息向公众提供；

(c) 控制者适用的欧盟法或成员国法律明确规定了数据的获取或披露，且规定了保护数据主体正当利益的适当措施；或者

(d) 个人数据须根据欧盟法或成员国法律规定的职业保密义务保持秘密，包括法定保密义务。

<details id="gdpr-article-15">
<summary>第15条 数据主体的访问权 / Article 15 — Right of access by the data subject</summary>

**English**

1. The data subject shall have the right to obtain from the controller confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning him or her are being processed, and, where that is the case, access to the personal data and the following information:

(a) the purposes of the processing;

(b) the categories of personal data concerned;

(c) the recipients or categories of recipient to whom the personal data have been or will be disclosed, in particular recipients in third countries or international organisations;

(d) where possible, the envisaged period for which the personal data will be stored, or, if not possible, the criteria used to determine that period;

(e) the existence of the right to request from the controller rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction of processing of personal data concerning the data subject or to object to such processing;

(f) the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority;

(g) where the personal data are not collected from the data subject, any available information as to their source;

(h) the existence of automated decision-making, including profiling, referred to in Article 22(1) and (4) and, at least in those cases, meaningful information about the logic involved, as well as the significance and the envisaged consequences of such processing for the data subject.

2. Where personal data are transferred to a third country or to an international organisation, the data subject shall have the right to be informed of the appropriate safeguards pursuant to Article 46 relating to the transfer.

3. The controller shall provide a copy of the personal data undergoing processing. For any further copies requested by the data subject, the controller may charge a reasonable fee based on administrative costs. Where the data subject makes the request by electronic means, and unless otherwise requested by the data subject, the information shall be provided in a commonly used electronic form.

4. The right to obtain a copy referred to in paragraph 3 shall not adversely affect the rights and freedoms of others.

**中文参考译文**

1. 数据主体有权从控制者获得确认，知悉其个人数据是否正在被处理；正在被处理的，有权访问该等个人数据及下列信息：

(a) 处理目的；

(b) 所涉及的个人数据类别；

(c) 个人数据已经或将要披露给的接收方或接收方类别，特别是第三国或国际组织中的接收方；

(d) 可能时，个人数据预计保存的期限；无法确定的，提供确定该期限的标准；

(e) 有权请求控制者更正或删除与数据主体有关的个人数据、限制处理该等个人数据，或反对该处理；

(f) 向监管机构投诉的权利；

(g) 个人数据并非从数据主体收集的，关于其来源的任何可用信息；

(h) 是否存在第22条第1、4款所述自动化决策，包括画像；并至少在该等情况下，提供关于所涉及逻辑的有意义信息，以及该处理对数据主体的意义和预期后果。

2. 个人数据被传输至第三国或国际组织的，数据主体有权获知依据第46条与该传输有关的适当保障措施。

3. 控制者应提供正在处理的个人数据的副本。对于数据主体请求的额外副本，控制者可以根据行政成本收取合理费用。数据主体以电子方式提出请求且未另有请求的，应以通用电子形式提供信息。

4. 第3款所述获得副本的权利，不得对他人的权利和自由产生不利影响。

</details>

**第3节 更正与删除 / Section 3 — Rectification and erasure**

<details id="gdpr-article-16">
<summary>第16条 更正权 / Article 16 — Right to rectification</summary>

**English**

The data subject shall have the right to obtain from the controller without undue delay the rectification of inaccurate personal data concerning him or her. Taking into account the purposes of the processing, the data subject shall have the right to have incomplete personal data completed, including by means of providing a supplementary statement.

**中文参考译文**

数据主体有权要求控制者无不当迟延地更正与其有关的不准确个人数据。考虑处理目的，数据主体有权要求补全不完整的个人数据，包括通过提供补充声明的方式。

</details>

<details id="gdpr-article-17">
<summary>第17条 删除权（“被遗忘权”） / Article 17 — Right to erasure (‘right to be forgotten’)</summary>

**English**

1. The data subject shall have the right to obtain from the controller the erasure of personal data concerning him or her without undue delay and the controller shall have the obligation to erase personal data without undue delay where one of the following grounds applies:

(a) the personal data are no longer necessary in relation to the purposes for which they were collected or otherwise processed;

(b) the data subject withdraws consent on which the processing is based according to point (a) of Article 6(1), or point (a) of Article 9(2), and where there is no other legal ground for the processing;

(c) the data subject objects to the processing pursuant to Article 21(1) and there are no overriding legitimate grounds for the processing, or the data subject objects to the processing pursuant to Article 21(2);

(d) the personal data have been unlawfully processed;

(e) the personal data have to be erased for compliance with a legal obligation in Union or Member State law to which the controller is subject;

(f) the personal data have been collected in relation to the offer of information society services referred to in Article 8(1).

2. Where the controller has made the personal data public and is obliged pursuant to paragraph 1 to erase the personal data, the controller, taking account of available technology and the cost of implementation, shall take reasonable steps, including technical measures, to inform controllers which are processing the personal data that the data subject has requested the erasure by such controllers of any links to, or copy or replication of, those personal data.

3. Paragraphs 1 and 2 shall not apply to the extent that processing is necessary:

(a) for exercising the right of freedom of expression and information;

(b) for compliance with a legal obligation which requires processing by Union or Member State law to which the controller is subject or for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller;

(c) for reasons of public interest in the area of public health in accordance with points (h) and (i) of Article 9(2) as well as Article 9(3);

(d) for archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical research purposes or statistical purposes in accordance with Article 89(1) in so far as the right referred to in paragraph 1 is likely to render impossible or seriously impair the achievement of the objectives of that processing; or

(e) for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

**中文参考译文**

1. 有下列任一理由的，数据主体有权要求控制者无不当迟延地删除与其有关的个人数据，控制者也有义务无不当迟延地删除个人数据：

(a) 个人数据对于其收集或其他处理目的已不再必要；

(b) 数据主体撤回了作为处理依据的第6条第1款(a)项或第9条第2款(a)项同意，且该处理没有其他法律依据；

(c) 数据主体依据第21条第1款反对处理，且不存在优先于该反对的正当处理理由；或者数据主体依据第21条第2款反对处理；

(d) 个人数据已被非法处理；

(e) 为遵守控制者适用的欧盟法或成员国法律规定的义务，必须删除个人数据；

(f) 个人数据是在提供第8条第1款所述信息社会服务的情形下收集的。

2. 控制者已公开个人数据，且依据第1款有义务删除该等数据的，应考虑可用技术和实施成本，采取合理措施，包括技术措施，通知正在处理该等个人数据的控制者：数据主体已要求其删除指向该等个人数据的任何链接，或该等数据的副本或复制件。

3. 在处理为下列目的所必需的范围内，第1、2款不适用：

(a) 行使表达和信息自由权；

(b) 遵守控制者适用的欧盟法或成员国法律规定、要求进行处理的法律义务，或者执行公共利益任务，或行使赋予控制者的公权力；

(c) 依据第9条第2款(h)、(i)项及第9条第3款，为公共卫生领域的公共利益；

(d) 依据第89条第1款，为公共利益档案保存、科学或历史研究或统计目的，且第1款所述权利可能导致该处理目的无法实现或严重妨碍其实现；或者

(e) 确立、行使或抗辩法律请求。

</details>

<details id="gdpr-article-18">
<summary>第18条 限制处理权 / Article 18 — Right to restriction of processing</summary>

**English**

1. The data subject shall have the right to obtain from the controller restriction of processing where one of the following applies:

(a) the accuracy of the personal data is contested by the data subject, for a period enabling the controller to verify the accuracy of the personal data;

(b) the processing is unlawful and the data subject opposes the erasure of the personal data and requests the restriction of their use instead;

(c) the controller no longer needs the personal data for the purposes of the processing, but they are required by the data subject for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims;

(d) the data subject has objected to processing pursuant to Article 21(1) pending the verification whether the legitimate grounds of the controller override those of the data subject.

2. Where processing has been restricted under paragraph 1, such personal data shall, with the exception of storage, only be processed with the data subject's consent or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims or for the protection of the rights of another natural or legal person or for reasons of important public interest of the Union or of a Member State.

3. A data subject who has obtained restriction of processing pursuant to paragraph 1 shall be informed by the controller before the restriction of processing is lifted.

**中文参考译文**

1. 有下列任一情形的，数据主体有权要求控制者限制处理：

(a) 数据主体对个人数据准确性提出异议，在控制者核实个人数据准确性所需期间内；

(b) 处理违法，但数据主体反对删除个人数据，转而要求限制其使用；

(c) 控制者为处理目的已不再需要该等个人数据，但数据主体为确立、行使或抗辩法律请求仍需要该等数据；

(d) 数据主体已依据第21条第1款反对处理，尚待核实控制者的正当理由是否优先于数据主体的理由。

2. 依据第1款限制处理的，该等个人数据除存储外，仅可在数据主体同意，或为确立、行使或抗辩法律请求，或保护其他自然人或法人的权利，或基于欧盟或成员国重大公共利益的情况下进行处理。

3. 对依据第1款获准限制处理的数据主体，控制者应在解除处理限制之前予以告知。

</details>

<details id="gdpr-article-19">
<summary>第19条 更正、删除个人数据或限制处理的通知义务 / Article 19 — Notification obligation regarding rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction of processing</summary>

**English**

The controller shall communicate any rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction of processing carried out in accordance with Article 16, Article 17(1) and Article 18 to each recipient to whom the personal data have been disclosed, unless this proves impossible or involves disproportionate effort. The controller shall inform the data subject about those recipients if the data subject requests it.

**中文参考译文**

控制者应将依据第16条、第17条第1款和第18条所进行的任何个人数据更正、删除或处理限制，通知每一个已获披露个人数据的接收方，但经证明不可能或需要付出不相称努力的除外。数据主体提出请求的，控制者应将该等接收方的信息告知数据主体。

</details>

<details id="gdpr-article-20">
<summary>第20条 数据可携带权 / Article 20 — Right to data portability</summary>

**English**

1. The data subject shall have the right to receive the personal data concerning him or her, which he or she has provided to a controller, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have the right to transmit those data to another controller without hindrance from the controller to which the personal data have been provided, where:

(a) the processing is based on consent pursuant to point (a) of Article 6(1) or point (a) of Article 9(2) or on a contract pursuant to point (b) of Article 6(1); and

(b) the processing is carried out by automated means.

2. In exercising his or her right to data portability pursuant to paragraph 1, the data subject shall have the right to have the personal data transmitted directly from one controller to another, where technically feasible.

3. The exercise of the right referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be without prejudice to Article 17. That right shall not apply to processing necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller.

4. The right referred to in paragraph 1 shall not adversely affect the rights and freedoms of others.

**中文参考译文**

1. 在下列条件均满足时，数据主体有权以结构化、通用且可供机器读取的格式，接收其向控制者提供的与其有关的个人数据，并有权将该等数据传输给另一控制者，不受原接收个人数据的控制者阻碍：

(a) 处理基于第6条第1款(a)项或第9条第2款(a)项的同意，或基于第6条第1款(b)项的合同；并且

(b) 处理以自动化方式进行。

2. 数据主体依据第1款行使数据可携带权时，在技术可行的情况下，有权要求个人数据直接从一个控制者传输至另一控制者。

3. 行使本条第1款所述权利，不影响第17条。该权利不适用于为执行公共利益任务或行使赋予控制者的公权力所必需的处理。

4. 第1款所述权利不得对他人的权利和自由产生不利影响。

</details>

**第4节 反对权与自动化个体决策 / Section 4 — Right to object and automated individual decision-making**

<details id="gdpr-article-21">
<summary>第21条 反对权 / Article 21 — Right to object</summary>

**English**

1. The data subject shall have the right to object, on grounds relating to his or her particular situation, at any time to processing of personal data concerning him or her which is based on point (e) or (f) of Article 6(1), including profiling based on those provisions. The controller shall no longer process the personal data unless the controller demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override the interests, rights and freedoms of the data subject or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

2. Where personal data are processed for direct marketing purposes, the data subject shall have the right to object at any time to processing of personal data concerning him or her for such marketing, which includes profiling to the extent that it is related to such direct marketing.

3. Where the data subject objects to processing for direct marketing purposes, the personal data shall no longer be processed for such purposes.

4. At the latest at the time of the first communication with the data subject, the right referred to in paragraphs 1 and 2 shall be explicitly brought to the attention of the data subject and shall be presented clearly and separately from any other information.

5. In the context of the use of information society services, and notwithstanding Directive 2002/58/EC, the data subject may exercise his or her right to object by automated means using technical specifications.

6. Where personal data are processed for scientific or historical research purposes or statistical purposes pursuant to Article 89(1), the data subject, on grounds relating to his or her particular situation, shall have the right to object to processing of personal data concerning him or her, unless the processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out for reasons of public interest.

**中文参考译文**

1. 对于基于第6条第1款(e)或(f)项进行的与其有关的个人数据处理，包括基于该等规定进行的画像，数据主体有权基于其特定情况随时提出反对。控制者不得再处理该等个人数据，除非其能够证明存在优先于数据主体利益、权利和自由的具有压倒性的正当处理理由，或者处理是为确立、行使或抗辩法律请求。

2. 为直接营销目的处理个人数据的，数据主体有权随时反对为该营销目的处理与其有关的个人数据；这包括与该直接营销有关范围内的画像。

3. 数据主体反对为直接营销目的进行处理的，不得再为该等目的处理个人数据。

4. 最迟在首次与数据主体沟通之时，应明确提请数据主体注意第1、2款所述权利，并将其与其他任何信息分开、清楚地呈现。

5. 在使用信息社会服务的情况下，不受第2002/58/EC号指令影响，数据主体可以采用技术规范，通过自动化方式行使反对权。

6. 依据第89条第1款，为科学或历史研究或统计目的处理个人数据的，数据主体有权基于其特定情况，反对处理与其有关的个人数据，但处理为执行基于公共利益的任务所必需的除外。

</details>

<details id="gdpr-article-22">
<summary>第22条 自动化个体决策，包括画像 / Article 22 — Automated individual decision-making, including profiling</summary>

**English**

1. The data subject shall have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning him or her or similarly significantly affects him or her.

2. Paragraph 1 shall not apply if the decision:

(a) is necessary for entering into, or performance of, a contract between the data subject and a data controller;

(b) is authorised by Union or Member State law to which the controller is subject and which also lays down suitable measures to safeguard the data subject's rights and freedoms and legitimate interests; or

(c) is based on the data subject's explicit consent.

3. In the cases referred to in points (a) and (c) of paragraph 2, the data controller shall implement suitable measures to safeguard the data subject's rights and freedoms and legitimate interests, at least the right to obtain human intervention on the part of the controller, to express his or her point of view and to contest the decision.

4. Decisions referred to in paragraph 2 shall not be based on special categories of personal data referred to in Article 9(1), unless point (a) or (g) of Article 9(2) applies and suitable measures to safeguard the data subject's rights and freedoms and legitimate interests are in place.

**中文参考译文**

1. 数据主体有权不受仅基于自动化处理（包括画像）作出、并对其产生法律效果或类似重大影响的决定约束。

2. 决定具有下列任一情形的，第1款不适用：

(a) 为订立或履行数据主体与数据控制者之间的合同所必需；

(b) 获控制者适用的欧盟法或成员国法律授权，且该法律同时规定了保障数据主体权利、自由和正当利益的适宜措施；或者

(c) 基于数据主体的明确同意。

3. 在第2款(a)、(c)项所述情况下，数据控制者应采取适宜措施，保障数据主体的权利、自由和正当利益，至少包括要求控制者进行人工干预、表达自身观点及对决定提出异议的权利。

4. 第2款所述决定不得基于第9条第1款所述特殊类别个人数据，除非适用第9条第2款(a)或(g)项，且已采取适宜措施保障数据主体的权利、自由和正当利益。

</details>

**第5节 限制 / Section 5 — Restrictions**

<details id="gdpr-article-23">
<summary>第23条 限制 / Article 23 — Restrictions</summary>

**English**

1. Union or Member State law to which the data controller or processor is subject may restrict by way of a legislative measure the scope of the obligations and rights provided for in Articles 12 to 22 and Article 34, as well as Article 5 in so far as its provisions correspond to the rights and obligations provided for in Articles 12 to 22, when such a restriction respects the essence of the fundamental rights and freedoms and is a necessary and proportionate measure in a democratic society to safeguard:

(a) national security;

(b) defence;

(c) public security;

(d) the prevention, investigation, detection or prosecution of criminal offences or the execution of criminal penalties, including the safeguarding against and the prevention of threats to public security;

(e) other important objectives of general public interest of the Union or of a Member State, in particular an important economic or financial interest of the Union or of a Member State, including monetary, budgetary and taxation a matters, public health and social security;

(f) the protection of judicial independence and judicial proceedings;

(g) the prevention, investigation, detection and prosecution of breaches of ethics for regulated professions;

(h) a monitoring, inspection or regulatory function connected, even occasionally, to the exercise of official authority in the cases referred to in points (a) to (e) and (g);

(i) the protection of the data subject or the rights and freedoms of others;

(j) the enforcement of civil law claims.

2. In particular, any legislative measure referred to in paragraph 1 shall contain specific provisions at least, where relevant, as to:

(a) the purposes of the processing or categories of processing;

(b) the categories of personal data;

(c) the scope of the restrictions introduced;

(d) the safeguards to prevent abuse or unlawful access or transfer;

(e) the specification of the controller or categories of controllers;

(f) the storage periods and the applicable safeguards taking into account the nature, scope and purposes of the processing or categories of processing;

(g) the risks to the rights and freedoms of data subjects; and

(h) the right of data subjects to be informed about the restriction, unless that may be prejudicial to the purpose of the restriction.

**中文参考译文**

1. 数据控制者或处理者适用的欧盟法或成员国法律，可以通过立法措施，限制第12至22条和第34条所规定义务与权利的范围，以及第5条中与第12至22条权利义务相对应的规定；但该限制必须尊重基本权利和自由的本质，并且是在民主社会中为保障下列事项所必需且相称的措施：

(a) 国家安全；

(b) 国防；

(c) 公共安全；

(d) 预防、调查、侦查或起诉刑事犯罪，或执行刑事处罚，包括防范和预防对公共安全的威胁；

(e) 欧盟或成员国其他重要的一般公共利益目标，特别是欧盟或成员国重要的经济或金融利益，包括货币、预算和税务事项，以及公共卫生和社会保障；

(f) 保护司法独立和司法程序；

(g) 预防、调查、侦查及追究受监管职业违反职业道德的行为；

(h) 与(a)至(e)项及(g)项所述情况下行使公权力有关的监测、检查或监管职能，即使该联系只是偶然存在；

(i) 保护数据主体或他人的权利和自由；

(j) 执行民事法律请求。

2. 特别是，第1款所述任何立法措施，至少应在相关情况下对下列事项作出具体规定：

(a) 处理目的或处理类别；

(b) 个人数据类别；

(c) 所引入限制的范围；

(d) 防止滥用、非法访问或非法传输的保障措施；

(e) 控制者或控制者类别的具体确定；

(f) 考虑处理或处理类别的性质、范围和目的而确定的保存期限及适用保障措施；

(g) 对数据主体权利和自由的风险；以及

(h) 数据主体被告知该限制的权利，但告知可能损害该限制目的的除外。

</details>
