### 序言第21—30段 / Recitals 21–30 {#dora-base-recitals-21-30}

<details>
<summary>序言（21）</summary>

**English**

(21) In order to maintain full control over ICT risk, financial entities need to have comprehensive capabilities to enable a strong and effective ICT risk management, as well as specific mechanisms and policies for handling all ICT-related incidents and for reporting major ICT-related incidents. Likewise, financial entities should have policies in place for the testing of ICT systems, controls and processes, as well as for managing ICT third-party risk. The digital operational resilience baseline for financial entities should be increased while also allowing for a proportionate application of requirements for certain financial entities, particularly microenterprises, as well as financial entities subject to a simplified ICT risk management framework. To facilitate an efficient supervision of institutions for occupational retirement provision that is proportionate and addresses the need to reduce administrative burdens on the competent authorities, the relevant national supervisory arrangements in respect of such financial entities should take into account their size and overall risk profile, and the nature, scale and complexity of their services, activities and operations even when the relevant thresholds established in Article 5 of Directive (EU) 2016/2341 of the European Parliament and of the Council [(10)](#dora-base-note-10) are exceeded. In particular, supervisory activities should focus primarily on the need to address serious risks associated with the ICT risk management of a particular entity. Competent authorities should also maintain a vigilant but proportionate approach in relation to the supervision of institutions for occupational retirement provision which, in accordance with Article 31 of Directive (EU) 2016/2341, outsource a significant part of their core business, such as asset management, actuarial calculations, accounting and data management, to service providers.

**中文参考译文**

（21）为保持对ICT风险的全面控制，金融实体需要具备全面能力，以开展有力、有效的ICT风险管理，并设置处理所有ICT相关事件及报告重大ICT相关事件的专门机制和政策。同样，金融实体应制定测试ICT系统、控制措施和流程，以及管理ICT第三方风险的政策。在提高金融实体数字运营韧性基线的同时，也应允许对某些金融实体，特别是微型企业及适用简化ICT风险管理框架的金融实体，按比例适用要求。为促进对职业退休金机构实施符合比例原则的有效监管，并满足减轻主管机关行政负担的需要，各国针对这类金融实体的相关监管安排应考虑其规模、整体风险状况，以及服务、活动和运营的性质、范围及复杂性，即使已经超过欧洲议会和理事会指令（EU）2016/2341[(10)](#dora-base-note-10)第5条规定的有关门槛。尤其是，监管活动应主要关注应对特定实体ICT风险管理相关严重风险的需要。对于依照指令（EU）2016/2341第31条，将资产管理、精算、会计和数据管理等核心业务的重要部分外包给服务提供者的职业退休金机构，主管机关也应保持审慎关注、同时符合比例原则的监管方法。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（22）</summary>

**English**

(22) ICT-related incident reporting thresholds and taxonomies vary significantly at national level. While common ground may be achieved through the relevant work undertaken by the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) established by Regulation (EU) 2019/881 of the European Parliament and of the Council [(11)](#dora-base-note-11) and the Cooperation Group under Directive (EU) 2022/2555, divergent approaches on setting the thresholds and use of taxonomies still exist, or can emerge, for the remainder of financial entities. Due to those divergences, there are multiple requirements that financial entities must comply with, especially when operating across several Member States and when part of a financial group. Moreover, such divergences have the potential to hinder the creation of further uniform or centralised Union mechanisms that speed up the reporting process and support a quick and smooth exchange of information between competent authorities, which is crucial for addressing ICT risk in the event of large-scale attacks with potentially systemic consequences.

**中文参考译文**

（22）各国ICT相关事件报告门槛及分类体系存在显著差异。由欧洲议会和理事会条例（EU）2019/881[(11)](#dora-base-note-11)设立的欧盟网络安全局（ENISA），以及指令（EU）2022/2555项下合作组开展的有关工作，虽可能促进形成共同基础，但针对其他金融实体，在门槛设定及分类体系使用方面仍存在或可能出现不同方法。这些差异导致金融实体必须遵守多重要求，在多个成员国经营以及属于金融集团时尤为如此。此外，这些差异还可能妨碍进一步建立统一或集中的欧盟机制，以加快报告流程并支持主管机关间迅速、顺畅地交换信息；在发生可能产生系统性后果的大规模攻击时，这类机制对于应对ICT风险至关重要。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（23）</summary>

**English**

(23) To reduce the administrative burden and potentially duplicative reporting obligations for certain financial entities, the requirement for the incident reporting pursuant to Directive (EU) 2015/2366 of the European Parliament and of the Council [(12)](#dora-base-note-12) should cease to apply to payment service providers that fall within the scope of this Regulation. Consequently, credit institutions, e-money institutions, payment institutions and account information service providers, as referred to in Article 33(1) of that Directive, should, from the date of application of this Regulation, report pursuant to this Regulation, all operational or security payment-related incidents which have been previously reported pursuant to that Directive, irrespective of whether such incidents are ICT-related.

**中文参考译文**

（23）为减少某些金融实体的行政负担及可能重复的报告义务，欧洲议会和理事会指令（EU）2015/2366[(12)](#dora-base-note-12)项下事件报告要求，应停止适用于本条例范围内的支付服务提供者。因此，自本条例适用之日起，信贷机构、电子货币机构、支付机构，以及该指令第33条第1款所述账户信息服务提供者，应依本条例报告此前依该指令报告的所有支付相关操作或安全事件，无论这些事件是否与ICT有关。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（24）</summary>

**English**

(24) To enable competent authorities to fulfil supervisory roles by acquiring a complete overview of the nature, frequency, significance and impact of ICT-related incidents and to enhance the exchange of information between relevant public authorities, including law enforcement authorities and resolution authorities, this Regulation should lay down a robust ICT-related incident reporting regime whereby the relevant requirements address current gaps in financial services law, and remove existing overlaps and duplications to alleviate costs. It is essential to harmonise the ICT-related incident reporting regime by requiring all financial entities to report to their competent authorities through a single streamlined framework as set out in this Regulation. In addition, the ESAs should be empowered to further specify relevant elements for the ICT-related incident reporting framework, such as taxonomy, timeframes, data sets, templates and applicable thresholds. To ensure full consistency with Directive (EU) 2022/2555, financial entities should be allowed, on a voluntary basis, to notify significant cyber threats to the relevant competent authority, when they consider that the cyber threat is of relevance to the financial system, service users or clients.

**中文参考译文**

（24）为使主管机关通过全面掌握ICT相关事件的性质、频率、重要程度及影响来履行监管职责，并加强包括执法机关和处置当局在内的有关公共机关间的信息交换，本条例应建立健全的ICT相关事件报告制度，以相关要求填补金融服务法律的现有空白，消除既有重叠和重复，从而减轻成本。必须通过要求所有金融实体按照本条例所设单一、精简的框架向主管机关报告，协调ICT相关事件报告制度。此外，应授权欧洲监管当局进一步明确ICT相关事件报告框架的相关要素，例如分类体系、时限、数据集、模板及适用门槛。为确保与指令（EU）2022/2555完全一致，当金融实体认为某项网络威胁与金融体系、服务使用者或客户有关时，应允许其自愿向有关主管机关通知重大网络威胁。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（25）</summary>

**English**

(25) Digital operational resilience testing requirements have been developed in certain financial subsectors setting out frameworks that are not always fully aligned. This leads to a potential duplication of costs for cross-border financial entities and makes the mutual recognition of the results of digital operational resilience testing complex which, in turn, can fragment the internal market.

**中文参考译文**

（25）部分金融子行业已经制定数字运营韧性测试要求，但其框架并不总是完全一致。这可能导致跨境金融实体重复承担成本，并使数字运营韧性测试结果的相互认可变得复杂，进而可能造成内部市场分割。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（26）</summary>

**English**

(26) In addition, where no ICT testing is required, vulnerabilities remain undetected and result in exposing a financial entity to ICT risk and ultimately create a higher risk to the stability and integrity of the financial sector. Without Union intervention, digital operational resilience testing would continue to be inconsistent and would lack a system of mutual recognition of ICT testing results across different jurisdictions. In addition, as it is unlikely that other financial subsectors would adopt testing schemes on a meaningful scale, they would miss out on the potential benefits of a testing framework, in terms of revealing ICT vulnerabilities and risks, and testing defence capabilities and business continuity, which contributes to increasing the trust of customers, suppliers and business partners. To remedy those overlaps, divergences and gaps, it is necessary to lay down rules for a coordinated testing regime and thereby facilitate the mutual recognition of advanced testing for financial entities meeting the criteria set out in this Regulation.

**中文参考译文**

（26）此外，在未要求开展ICT测试的情况下，脆弱性可能无法被发现，使金融实体暴露于ICT风险，最终对金融部门的稳定性和完整性造成更高风险。没有欧盟层面的干预，数字运营韧性测试将继续不一致，并缺乏跨司法管辖区相互认可ICT测试结果的制度。其他金融子行业也不太可能大规模采用有实质意义的测试方案，因此将无法获得测试框架在揭示ICT脆弱性和风险、检验防御能力及业务连续性方面的潜在益处，而这些益处有助于增进客户、供应商和业务伙伴的信任。为纠正上述重叠、分歧和空白，有必要制定协调测试制度的规则，从而促进对符合本条例所定标准的金融实体所开展高级测试的相互认可。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（27）</summary>

**English**

(27) Financial entities’ reliance on the use of ICT services is partly driven by their need to adapt to an emerging competitive digital global economy, to boost their business efficiency and to meet consumer demand. The nature and extent of such reliance has been continuously evolving in recent years, driving cost reduction in financial intermediation, enabling business expansion and scalability in the deployment of financial activities while offering a wide range of ICT tools to manage complex internal processes.

**中文参考译文**

（27）金融实体对ICT服务使用的依赖，部分源于适应新兴、竞争性的数字化全球经济、提高业务效率以及满足消费者需求的需要。近年来，这种依赖的性质和程度持续演变，推动金融中介成本下降，使业务扩张及金融活动部署的规模化成为可能，同时提供广泛的ICT工具来管理复杂内部流程。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（28）</summary>

**English**

(28) The extensive use of ICT services is evidenced by complex contractual arrangements, whereby financial entities often encounter difficulties in negotiating contractual terms that are tailored to the prudential standards or other regulatory requirements to which they are subject, or otherwise in enforcing specific rights, such as access or audit rights, even when the latter are enshrined in their contractual arrangements. Moreover, many of those contractual arrangements do not provide for sufficient safeguards allowing for the fully-fledged monitoring of subcontracting processes, thus depriving the financial entity of its ability to assess the associated risks. In addition, as ICT third-party service providers often provide standardised services to different types of clients, such contractual arrangements do not always cater adequately for the individual or specific needs of financial industry actors.

**中文参考译文**

（28）复杂的合同安排体现了ICT服务的广泛使用。金融实体经常难以通过协商获得符合其所受审慎标准或其他监管要求的合同条款，或者难以行使访问权、审计权等特定权利，即使这些权利已被写入合同。此外，许多此类合同安排缺乏足够保障，不能支持对分包过程的全面监测，因而使金融实体无法评估相关风险。ICT第三方服务提供者还经常向不同类型客户提供标准化服务，因此这些合同安排并不总能充分满足金融行业参与者的个别或特殊需要。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（29）</summary>

**English**

(29) Even though Union financial services law contains certain general rules on outsourcing, monitoring of the contractual dimension is not fully anchored into Union law. In the absence of clear and bespoke Union standards applying to the contractual arrangements concluded with ICT third-party service providers, the external source of ICT risk is not comprehensively addressed. Consequently, it is necessary to set out certain key principles to guide financial entities’ management of ICT third-party risk, which are of particular importance when financial entities resort to ICT third-party service providers to support their critical or important functions. Those principles should be accompanied by a set of core contractual rights in relation to several elements in the performance and termination of contractual arrangements with a view to providing certain minimum safeguards in order to strengthen financial entities’ ability to effectively monitor all ICT risk emerging at the level of third-party service providers. Those principles are complementary to the sectoral law applicable to outsourcing.

**中文参考译文**

（29）虽然欧盟金融服务法律包含某些外包一般规则，合同层面的监测尚未在欧盟法律中得到充分确立。由于缺少适用于与ICT第三方服务提供者订立合同安排的明确、有针对性的欧盟标准，ICT风险的外部来源未被全面处理。因此，有必要规定若干关键原则，指导金融实体管理ICT第三方风险；当金融实体利用ICT第三方服务提供者支持其关键或重要功能时，这些原则尤其重要。这些原则应配套一组涉及合同安排履行和终止若干要素的核心合同权利，以提供一定最低保障，增强金融实体有效监测第三方服务提供者层面产生的所有ICT风险的能力。这些原则是适用于外包的行业法律的补充。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（30）</summary>

**English**

(30) A certain lack of homogeneity and convergence regarding the monitoring of ICT third-party risk and ICT third-party dependencies is evident today. Despite efforts to address outsourcing, such as EBA Guidelines on outsourcing of 2019 and ESMA Guidelines on outsourcing to cloud service providers of 2021 the broader issue of counteracting systemic risk which may be triggered by the financial sector’s exposure to a limited number of critical ICT third-party service providers is not sufficiently addressed by Union law. The lack of rules at Union level is compounded by the absence of national rules on mandates and tools that allow financial supervisors to acquire a good understanding of ICT third-party dependencies and to monitor adequately risks arising from the concentration of ICT third-party dependencies.

**中文参考译文**

（30）目前，ICT第三方风险及ICT第三方依赖关系的监测明显存在一定程度的统一性和趋同性不足。尽管已有2019年EBA外包指南和2021年ESMA向云服务提供者外包指南等应对外包问题的努力，欧盟法律仍未充分处理更广泛的问题，即应对金融部门因依赖数量有限的关键ICT第三方服务提供者而可能引发的系统性风险。欧盟层面规则的缺乏，还因各国未制定相关职权与工具规则而进一步加剧；这些规则本应使金融监管者能够充分了解ICT第三方依赖，并适当监测ICT第三方依赖集中产生的风险。

</details>

### 序言第31—40段 / Recitals 31–40 {#dora-base-recitals-31-40}

<details>
<summary>序言（31）</summary>

**English**

(31) Taking into account the potential systemic risk entailed by increased outsourcing practices and by the ICT third-party concentration, and mindful of the insufficiency of national mechanisms in providing financial supervisors with adequate tools to quantify, qualify and redress the consequences of ICT risk occurring at critical ICT third-party service providers, it is necessary to establish an appropriate Oversight Framework allowing for a continuous monitoring of the activities of ICT third-party service providers that are critical ICT third-party service providers to financial entities, while ensuring that the confidentiality and security of customers other than financial entities is preserved. While intra-group provision of ICT services entails specific risks and benefits, it should not be automatically considered less risky than the provision of ICT services by providers outside of a financial group and should therefore be subject to the same regulatory framework. However, when ICT services are provided from within the same financial group, financial entities might have a higher level of control over intra-group providers, which ought to be taken into account in the overall risk assessment.

**中文参考译文**

（31）考虑到日益增加的外包实践和ICT第三方集中所带来的潜在系统性风险，以及各国机制尚不足以向金融监管者提供适当工具，对关键ICT第三方服务提供者发生的ICT风险后果进行定量、定性分析和补救，有必要建立适当监督框架，对向金融实体提供关键ICT第三方服务的提供者持续开展活动监测，同时确保金融实体以外客户的保密性和安全得到保护。集团内部提供ICT服务虽有特殊风险和益处，也不应自动被认为比集团外部提供者提供ICT服务的风险更低，因此应适用相同监管框架。然而，在同一金融集团内部提供ICT服务时，金融实体可能对集团内部提供者具有更高控制程度，整体风险评估应考虑这一点。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（32）</summary>

**English**

(32) With ICT risk becoming more and more complex and sophisticated, good measures for the detection and prevention of ICT risk depend to a great extent on the regular sharing between financial entities of threat and vulnerability intelligence. Information sharing contributes to creating increased awareness of cyber threats. In turn, this enhances the capacity of financial entities to prevent cyber threats from becoming real ICT-related incidents and enables financial entities to more effectively contain the impact of ICT-related incidents and to recover faster. In the absence of guidance at Union level, several factors seem to have inhibited such intelligence sharing, in particular uncertainty about its compatibility with data protection, anti-trust and liability rules.

**中文参考译文**

（32）随着ICT风险日益复杂和精巧，良好的ICT风险检测与预防措施在很大程度上依赖金融实体定期共享威胁及脆弱性情报。信息共享有助于提高网络威胁意识，进而增强金融实体防止网络威胁演变为实际ICT相关事件的能力，使其更有效地遏制事件影响并更快恢复。由于缺少欧盟层面的指引，一些因素似乎阻碍了这种情报共享，尤其是对其是否符合数据保护、反垄断和责任规则的不确定性。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（33）</summary>

**English**

(33) In addition, doubts about the type of information that can be shared with other market participants, or with non-supervisory authorities (such as ENISA, for analytical input, or Europol, for law enforcement purposes) lead to useful information being withheld. Therefore, the extent and quality of information sharing currently remains limited and fragmented, with relevant exchanges mostly being local (by way of national initiatives) and with no consistent Union-wide information-sharing arrangements tailored to the needs of an integrated financial system. It is therefore important to strengthen those communication channels.

**中文参考译文**

（33）此外，对于可以与其他市场参与者，或者与非监管机关共享哪些类型的信息，仍存在疑虑，例如向ENISA提供分析材料，或者为执法目的向欧洲刑警组织提供信息，致使有用信息未被共享。因此，目前信息共享的范围和质量仍有限且零散，相关交流多为地方性的，主要通过各国举措进行，缺少适应一体化金融体系需要、覆盖整个欧盟的一致信息共享安排。因此，有必要强化这些沟通渠道。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（34）</summary>

**English**

(34) Financial entities should be encouraged to exchange among themselves cyber threat information and intelligence, and to collectively leverage their individual knowledge and practical experience at strategic, tactical and operational levels with a view to enhancing their capabilities to adequately assess, monitor, defend against, and respond to cyber threats, by participating in information sharing arrangements. It is therefore necessary to enable the emergence at Union level of mechanisms for voluntary information-sharing arrangements which, when conducted in trusted environments, would help the community of the financial industry to prevent and collectively respond to cyber threats by quickly limiting the spread of ICT risk and impeding potential contagion throughout the financial channels. Those mechanisms should comply with the applicable competition law rules of the Union set out in the Communication from the Commission of 14 January 2011 entitled ‘Guidelines on the applicability of Article 101 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union to horizontal cooperation agreements’, as well as with Union data protection rules, in particular Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council [(13)](#dora-base-note-13). They should operate based on the use of one or more of the legal bases that are laid down in Article 6 of that Regulation, such as in the context of the processing of personal data that is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interest pursued by the controller or by a third party, as referred to in Article 6(1), point (f), of that Regulation, as well as in the context of the processing of personal data necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject, necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller, as referred to in Article 6(1), points (c) and (e), respectively, of that Regulation.

**中文参考译文**

（34）应鼓励金融实体参与信息共享安排，相互交换网络威胁信息和情报，并在战略、战术和运营层面共同利用各自知识与实践经验，以增强其适当评估、监测、防御和响应网络威胁的能力。因此，有必要推动在欧盟层面形成自愿信息共享安排机制。在可信环境中运行这些机制，可以通过迅速限制ICT风险传播、阻断风险沿金融渠道潜在蔓延，帮助金融行业共同体预防并集体应对网络威胁。这些机制应遵守欧盟委员会2011年1月14日题为《关于〈欧洲联盟运行条约〉第101条适用于横向合作协议的指南》的通报所载适用欧盟竞争法规则，以及欧盟数据保护规则，特别是欧洲议会和理事会条例（EU）2016/679[(13)](#dora-base-note-13)。这些机制应基于该条例第6条规定的一项或多项法律基础运行，例如该条例第6条第1款(f)项所述，为控制者或第三方追求的正当利益所必需的个人数据处理，以及该条例第6条第1款(c)项和(e)项分别所述，为履行控制者所承担法律义务所必需的处理，或为执行公共利益任务、行使赋予控制者的公权力所必需的处理。

</details>

<details>
<summary>序言（35）</summary>

**English**

(35) In order to maintain a high level of digital operational resilience for the whole financial sector, and at the same time to keep pace with technological developments, this Regulation should address risk stemming from all types of ICT services. To that end, the definition of ICT services in the context of this Regulation should be understood in a broad manner, encompassing digital and data services provided through ICT systems to one or more internal or external users on an ongoing basis. That definition should, for instance, include so called ‘over the top’ services, which fall within the category of electronic communications services. It should exclude only the limited category of traditional analogue telephone services qualifying as Public Switched Telephone Network (PSTN) services, landline services, Plain Old Telephone Service (POTS), or fixed-line telephone services.

**中文参考译文**

（35）为使整个金融部门保持高水平数字运营韧性，同时跟上技术发展，本条例应处理所有类型ICT服务产生的风险。为此，应对本条例语境下的ICT服务定义作广义理解，涵盖通过ICT系统持续向一个或多个内部或外部使用者提供的数字和数据服务。例如，该定义应包括属于电子通信服务类别的所谓“OTT”服务。应仅排除有限类别的传统模拟电话服务，即公共交换电话网络（PSTN）服务、陆地线路服务、传统普通电话服务（POTS）或固定线路电话服务。
